How it works & privacy

Where your recording goes. The audio is recorded in this browser and is uploaded only to Gradescope, with the rest of your submission. It is not sent anywhere else, and this page never uploads it to the quiz server — that server only ever receives a fingerprint (hash) of the audio and some timestamps, which is what lets it certify that you answered live without hearing a word of it.

What happens after you upload. Inside Gradescope, the audio is turned into text by software running there. The text of your answer is then sent to Purdue’s GenAI Studio to be graded against the rubric; the audio never is. Your instructor can listen to the audio and read the transcript.

Why we ask you not to say personal details. Anything you say becomes part of that transcript. Keeping the answer to the question keeps everything else out of it. If personal details do slip in, the grader removes what it can recognise before grading and tells your instructor — but the surest fix is not to say them.
