## Acknowledgements

Built with [MAGE](docs/project/MAGE.md).

The system is designed around tools institutions already provide, rather than adding another one.

Local grading and transcription use open-weights models (Whisper for speech recognition, and a
small instruction-tuned model for grading). These are downloaded at image-build time and are not
redistributed with this repository; each remains under its own licence.

## How to cite

See [`CITATION.cff`](CITATION.cff). If you use OralScale in teaching, an acknowledgement or a link
